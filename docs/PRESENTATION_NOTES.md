# Presentation notes and reconciliation

Recorded from the user's notes on 10 October 2026. These are recollections, not API specifications.

## Product motivation

Manufacturers buy many components for complex products. Procurement staff cannot negotiate personally with every supplier. An autonomous buyer can research supplier history and conduct the full negotiation, reserving human attention for other work. Tacto supplies the opposing salesperson agents; we build the buyer. One arena key is issued per team.

## Strategy ideas from the presentation

- Understand the supplier's importance, history, financial strength and constraints.
- Seek favorable prices while preserving a viable relationship and avoiding unnecessary walkaways.
- Use documented weaknesses, competing offers, quality issues and cost inconsistencies as negotiating arguments.
- Explore longer relationships and future business as things the supplier might value.
- Adapt the discussion to the supplier rather than use a fixed script.

## Differences from the inspected written rules

| Presentation recollection | Written rules / planning consequence |
|---|---|
| Cheapest price always wins; scoring is only price | Scoring uses total cost: coverage, payment, freight, delivery, quality, tooling, index risk and sometimes supplier health. Optimize the complete package. |
| One supplier per negotiation | Some cases have one; RFQs have two or three simultaneous supplier threads. Accepting one ends the entire case. |
| Talk until the supplier leaves | Usually ten buyer messages per thread, 60 seconds per decision, and an eight-minute final-wave cap. Close before losing a good standing offer. |
| Keep the supplier unhappy enough without quitting | No measured unhappiness objective. Model responsiveness and walkaway risk; sustainable margins explicitly matter in strategic/bottleneck cases. |
| Promise many future deals / a long partnership | Explore case-permitted volume and duration trades. Prose is not binding; term sheets are. Do not assume unlimited volume or make unsupported commitments. |
| Use supplier weaknesses | Use evidence and economically credible trades. True evidence does not guarantee a concession, and opponents have hidden floors and approval limits. |

The pinned written contract is our working basis. If the organizers changed the rules during the presentation, confirm the revision before changing the scoring implementation.
